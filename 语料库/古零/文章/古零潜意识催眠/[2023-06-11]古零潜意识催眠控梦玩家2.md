# 古零潜意识催眠-控梦玩家2
今天我们来到【[古零潜意识催眠-控梦玩家](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484731&idx=1&sn=c92a4bd881eb40c393316b1723b51748)
】第2集。[上一集](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484731&idx=1&sn=c92a4bd881eb40c393316b1723b51748)
我们讲到第一层梦往往是[个体幻相界](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247494103&idx=1&sn=114f55505dc9e565ff699b81347909fb)
，主要造幻的源头是个体自己，今天我们聊聊[第二层梦](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247496156&idx=1&sn=ddd1768d1f11c60ae1fb3bcdfe7361ad)
，它往往是[集体幻相界](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484517&idx=1&sn=17a9dfe7e3c85426b9b19260d1c49e55)
，造幻的源头是[集体意识](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247494106&idx=1&sn=2681e77dc55d36f34e330f01958e3c84)
。
那如何从第一层梦进入第二层梦呢？我们分6步走。
第1步养成[记梦](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247490168&idx=1&sn=8e8a641837d6114868fed83dc8848fdd)
的习惯，如果记不起来，可以通过[古零潜意识催眠](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484525&idx=1&sn=6bf098bf3a3078a90132cfe312a80cef)
，重新在相应的[梦境维度](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247492548&idx=1&sn=3ddbc26e6c2a21089a87d49c26e5634f)
下载记忆。第2步是养成[疑梦](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247494502&idx=1&sn=3e593e2555d1467eac5fc93a9c1e6892)
的习惯。第3步[验梦](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247496027&idx=1&sn=5c82a90bb009426389edffc7804b2b01)
。第4步[知梦](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247496156&idx=1&sn=ddd1768d1f11c60ae1fb3bcdfe7361ad)
。第5步，[控梦](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247491084&idx=1&sn=946fbd53b49907ad3122c7ec875e379f)
，控梦练习中可以借助[锚点](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484541&idx=1&sn=6c59560700123d8b2d666208f8eac2de)
重回梦境，简称断线[续梦](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247494502&idx=1&sn=3e593e2555d1467eac5fc93a9c1e6892)
。当你[控梦](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247496029&idx=1&sn=09cccc1178c9e41ee4f45e10b747ba54)
越来越成熟，就正式成为了[控梦玩家](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484731&idx=1&sn=c92a4bd881eb40c393316b1723b51748)
，也叫[控梦师](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247496029&idx=1&sn=09cccc1178c9e41ee4f45e10b747ba54)
，这个时候可以进入第6步，[梦修](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484263&idx=1&sn=a3e3f61009f85a837335fc12f9eecaa1)
，正式开启探索[本源](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484401&idx=1&sn=f1db0b5780dfd04d59ddfa4f4f81b20b)
之旅，也是自己从控梦玩家蜕变到[梦修](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247496156&idx=1&sn=ddd1768d1f11c60ae1fb3bcdfe7361ad)
玩家的开始。
