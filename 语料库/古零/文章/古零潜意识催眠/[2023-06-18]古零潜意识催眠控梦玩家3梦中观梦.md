# 古零潜意识催眠-控梦玩家3：梦中观梦
上一集我们分享了[梦修六步骤](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484761&idx=1&sn=71af154432ff9fd793dc8f2bdf27c3c4)
，这一期我们来讲讲如何在【[梦](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484731&idx=1&sn=c92a4bd881eb40c393316b1723b51748)
中[观梦](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484694&idx=1&sn=f3e1ef6867e1bcec08afed8d5ca9ef27)
】，一层层突破[梦界](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484263&idx=1&sn=a3e3f61009f85a837335fc12f9eecaa1)
去探索[本源](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484407&idx=1&sn=731b88a1771179d8c85e407a820525d9)
：

1、纯旁观停止造幻
首先在梦里要停止[造幻](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247489713&idx=1&sn=a7af48fce749c87b26606eb052216b54)
，让自己成为[纯旁观](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247489461&idx=1&sn=98ea0b3c9bb29622aabca1bf5f849e0a)
者，纯客观的观察着[梦境](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247489443&idx=1&sn=bfd02ac24430f6fe67fa0cadc16dc7be)
的一切，要做到这一步，需要先做到[沉寂小我](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484724&idx=1&sn=83c7c057c9376758afd1fd356508e7cd)
，即“无我”的状态，然后把自己的意识调整为[纯客观](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484307&idx=1&sn=0536ba2da5fe022d194964392579bb69)
状态，也可以称为[潜意识状态](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484282&idx=1&sn=3d0cd2dd6179ce5a83eb9ec43ec9faec)
。
【[古零潜意识催眠](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484106&idx=1&sn=4eecb18768e235ee65d59f0825122e58)
】有专门针对【[沉寂小我](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484375&idx=1&sn=b5bb84df314892cfc3820cb852e69d57)
】和【[潜意识状态转换](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484558&idx=1&sn=4dfbfbf4455d47cf8aa34c53d564229f)
】的练习，这些练习在你的日常生活中就能锻炼。

2、纯客观观察梦境，进入集体幻相界
当你开始纯客观的观察[梦境](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484197&idx=1&sn=7ecbf7738917d8a0fa232644b6779ba3)
，梦境就会开始发生各种变化，此时你不去跟随梦境的变化而起心动念，只保持如如不动的[观察](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484646&idx=1&sn=cdd62566ada46e70dd4ca35c42c676fe)
就可以了。
你的心保持不动，始终保持[零执着](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484240&idx=1&sn=91103c093d71af40db363bf01baf054b)
，[不当真](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484239&idx=1&sn=4860d17166cc98758872e86981418ab1)
的状态，并以[集体意识](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247494216&idx=1&sn=5cc8310206cc248580e5600b944a5139)
的多个体[多视角](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484307&idx=1&sn=0536ba2da5fe022d194964392579bb69)
去进行观察，客观客观再[客观](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484478&idx=1&sn=698d888951f79cf05a1797657bab6f1c)
，梦境的[幻相](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484453&idx=1&sn=022c6d7ae64abefbd859ad7a7aeac437)
就会慢慢消失，露出创造该[梦境](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484290&idx=1&sn=d3fdec5da0204dbadf2f0953c359d3d2)
的源头，这个时候[梦境](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247496027&idx=1&sn=5c82a90bb009426389edffc7804b2b01)
就进入了第2层梦——[集体幻相](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247494106&idx=1&sn=2681e77dc55d36f34e330f01958e3c84)
界
这个集体幻相界与个体幻相界最大的区别是，幻相比较稳固，个体不容易改变，而且时空更复杂，互动性更多，更大。

3、纯客观继续观察，探索本源
然后你保持这个[纯客观](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484282&idx=1&sn=3d0cd2dd6179ce5a83eb9ec43ec9faec)
的状态继续观察，会发现这个[集体幻相](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247494216&idx=1&sn=5cc8310206cc248580e5600b944a5139)
界其实也是被创造的，它的背后还有一个[源头](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484401&idx=1&sn=f1db0b5780dfd04d59ddfa4f4f81b20b)
，而且这个源头的频率更宽广，更自由，更精细，这时梦境其实已经跟[维度](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247491803&idx=1&sn=5aa357c07e4263c90d6f3c69476c7af6)
接轨了，越深的[梦境维度](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247492548&idx=1&sn=3ddbc26e6c2a21089a87d49c26e5634f)
就越高，并以层层嵌套延伸出去，上一界创造下一界，上一界比下一界更大，更精细，而且随着[维度](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247492841&idx=1&sn=c1c14b419550a6a5b2b7223742e57c20)
的提升，所在的[梦境](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484761&idx=1&sn=71af154432ff9fd793dc8f2bdf27c3c4)
从具象化延伸到抽象化。
只要你继续保持[纯客观](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484307&idx=1&sn=0536ba2da5fe022d194964392579bb69)
的状态，一直探索下去，就能破开一层一层的[幻相](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484453&idx=1&sn=022c6d7ae64abefbd859ad7a7aeac437)
，同时你[合一本源](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484407&idx=1&sn=731b88a1771179d8c85e407a820525d9)
的深度也会不断提升，到了最后就能进入[本源界](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484701&idx=1&sn=c9e030653457551436fd093b0213d57f)
，看到一个无限的、不生不灭的[自由意识](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzkwMTQwMzExNQ%3D%3D&mid=2247484273&idx=1&sn=bb5a1d674b70b177d8fff41ad30ca499)
，有着无穷无尽的自发幻想，其景象就是无数多样的宇宙和万物及各自的规则和不规则，这也充分体现出
从本源的角度，是用主观想象力创造万物，就是万物至繁，而我们是用客观观察力[逆行](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?biz=MzAxMzg5MDI2MA%3D%3D&mid=2247492841&idx=1&sn=c1c14b419550a6a5b2b7223742e57c20)
返回本源，就是大道至简。
